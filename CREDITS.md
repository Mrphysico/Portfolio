# CREDITS & ATTRIBUTIONS

## "THE WORKSHOP" & DIMENSIONAL PORTFOLIO

### 1. 3D Procedural Character ("Arth the Builder")
- **Author & Design**: Procedurally modeled in Three.js and React Three Fiber for Arth Jadav's Dimensional Portfolio.
- **Components**:
  - Stylized low-poly character with oversized dark navy hoodie and cyan accents.
  - Flip-up dual-lens tech goggles (amber & cyan lenses).
  - Studio headphones around the neck with glowing cyan sound rings.
  - Precision magnetic screwdriver.
  - Real-time inverse kinematics & procedural animation loops: idle breathing, pointer eye/head tracking, screw-fastening gesture, greeting wave, and celebratory fist-pump.
- **License**: MIT / Proprietary to Arth Jadav.
